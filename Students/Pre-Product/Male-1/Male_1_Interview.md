## 3. Difficulties While Reading and Writing

**Hamiz:** Okay, alright, alright. Do you face any difficulties while reading and writing? And if you do, could you tell us what kind of difficulties they are?

## 4. Smaller Content Sections

**Hamiz:** Okay, and what if we make the content section smaller? Like, for example, you are reading a book and one chapter is quite large. Personally, if I were asked, I would find it difficult to read. I would say, "Man, what is this, such a huge chapter." But if, instead, we divide it into five smaller chapters, then I would feel okay, like they will get finished one by one. So what are your thoughts on this? Which approach is better for you?

**Hamiz:** The smaller ones? Hmm, right, right, right. Okay.

## 5. White Noise

**Hamiz:** Hmm, okay. What are your thoughts on white noise? Actually, we asked one or two other students and they said white noise helps a lot. What is your opinion on this?

**Hassaan:** I didn't see any benefit from it.

---

## 6. Animations During Study

**Hamiz:** How do the animations on the left side feel? I mean, do they feel distracting while studying, like, "Man, what is this, get rid of it, animations keep popping up," or do they feel fine?

**Hassaan:** They bother me a lot, and my attention gets diverted elsewhere.

**Hamiz:** Okay, they bother you. Can you tell us in what way they bother you? I mean, what feels bad about it?

**Hassaan:** A person can't focus properly. The thing a person wants to focus on, alright... and then from behind, through an animation, the question is changed, or something else is done, or another chapter is opened through animations. So then it's not that much... it should be straightforward, it shouldn't be in every single thing. Right.

**Hamiz:** Hmm, okay, okay.

---

## 7. Mascot and Encouragement

**Hamiz:** Also, tell me this, what if there was a mascot on the platform? Like, if you do a good job, it praises you and encourages you like, "Wow, great job, keep it up!" How would that feel?

**Hassaan:** It feels distracting to me. In my opinion, it's much better at the bottom, a quiz-type thing should be made, like if there are ten questions, it should be done in a way that if you answer a question correctly, a bar is formed showing how many questions are right and how many are wrong. If that happens, it's much better. That cute type of platform bothers me a lot.

## 8. Quiz Results and Mistake Identification

**Hamiz:** Okay, so in the questions too... the quiz you're mentioning, you also want the quiz result to not just show you if you passed or failed, but to also show you that you got these specific questions right, and these specific ones wrong. So that mistakes can be identified from it.

**Hamiz:** Hmm. Right, right, right, right, okay.

## 9. Guest Mode and Login Options

**Hamiz:** And what if there is a guest mode on the site? Because we've often seen kids have a big problem with this, they say, "Man, what is this login page, get rid of it, I don't even want to go to the website." So if we add a guest mode so that you can go in without logging in, how would that be?

**Hassaan:** The only issue with guest mode is that in between, when you have to close the website, all your history gets wiped out from there. That is the meaning of guest mode, right? And in my opinion, both guest mode and the login page should be included. I mean, both choices should be there at the same time.
