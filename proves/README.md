# Type2Learn — Proof of Reach and Momentum

This folder preserves two dashboard snapshots that document Type2Learn’s early public reach, product engagement, international visibility, and delivery infrastructure.

The strongest message is simple: Type2Learn is no longer only an idea or a private prototype. These snapshots show thousands of users and visitors interacting with the platform, traffic arriving from multiple countries, and a live site handling real network activity.

## The headline numbers

### Cloudflare: live web reach and infrastructure

The Cloudflare export, captured on **9 August 2026 at 16:27**, reports:

- **2.07K total unique visitors** over the previous 30 days.
- **242 maximum unique visitors in a single day** during the displayed period.
- **31,843 SSL requests served** in the Share Stats panel.
- **398 MB of data saved** through Cloudflare in the last month.
- **70 attacks blocked** by the Cloudflare-protected site.
- In the visible previous-24-hour country panel: **4,786 requests from Pakistan**, **552 from Lithuania**, **271 from the United States**, **158 from the Netherlands**, and **112 from China**.

This is meaningful infrastructure evidence: people are reaching the production domain, requests are being served securely, bandwidth is being optimized, and the edge layer is filtering hostile traffic.

### Google Analytics: user activity and product interest

The Google Analytics Reports Snapshot covers **12 July–8 August 2026**, labelled as the last 28 days. It reports:

- **8.3K active users**.
- **8.3K new users**.
- **1 minute 40 seconds average engagement time per active user**.
- **23K page views**.
- **12K user-engagement events**.
- **11K session starts**.
- **8.3K first visits**.

The most-viewed screens shown in the snapshot include the main Type2Learn experience, the accessible landing page, and the learn-by-typing flow. That combination suggests interest is reaching beyond the homepage into the product’s learning experience.

## What this proves — and what it does not

These snapshots support four defensible conclusions:

1. **There is real early demand.** Thousands of users and visitors reached Type2Learn during the captured periods.
2. **People are interacting with the product.** Page views, sessions, engagement events, and time spent go beyond a single isolated visit.
3. **The product has international visibility.** Both reports show activity across multiple countries.
4. **The production site is handling real traffic.** Cloudflare records secure requests, bandwidth savings, and blocked attacks.

They do not, by themselves, prove revenue, conversion rate, long-term retention, learning outcomes, or audited user counts. Those would require additional reports and a consistent measurement methodology.

## Why the numbers should not be added together

The Google Analytics and Cloudflare figures are complementary, not additive. They use different systems, definitions, time windows, privacy controls, and counting methods:

- Google Analytics reports browser-level users, sessions, events, and engagement.
- Cloudflare reports edge-level visitors, requests, bandwidth, and security activity.
- The Google Analytics window is **12 July–8 August 2026**.
- The Cloudflare panels mix a **previous-30-day** view with a **previous-24-hour** country view.

For that reason, **8.3K plus 2.07K should not be presented as 10.37K unique people**. The accurate claim is that the two independent systems each recorded substantial activity around Type2Learn during their respective snapshot windows.
