## What These Reviews Collectively Show

### Reassurance
Reviewers consistently recognized Type2Learn as:
- **Clean, simple, and easy to navigate** across various desktop and mobile platforms.
- **Highly practical and student-friendly** for structured typing practice and focus retention.
- **Empathetic and supportive** of neurodivergent learning needs.
- **Flexible** due to multi-language support (Urdu and English) and customization options.

### Constructive Priorities
- **Value Communication:** Clearly explain accessibility and neurodivergent features to first-time visitors.
- **Navigation & Discovery:** Optimize site hierarchy so course sections are immediately discoverable.
- **Mobile Experience & Input:** Fix mobile typing behaviors, including native auto-fill interactions and WPM tracking limits.
- **Cognitive Load Management:** Address feedback regarding punctuation/typing mechanics competing with reading comprehension.
- **Content Expansion:** Continuously add curated courses while maintaining current design simplicity.
